How a review works

  1. 1

    Detect

    Finds names, contact details, identifiers, and indirect clues such as job title or city.

  2. 2

    Assess risk

    Weighs each item in context against the privacy policy.

  3. 3

    Redact

    Masks, generalises or pseudonymises each item with deterministic rules.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Re-scans the output independently and confirms the originals are gone.

  5. 5

    Attack

    Tries to re-identify people, within and across documents.

Only documents that pass every stage are released.
